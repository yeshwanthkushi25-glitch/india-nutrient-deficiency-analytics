Official Public Health Data Sources & Standards
  • ICMR-NIN (2024): Dietary Guidelines for Indians, National Institute of Nutrition, Indian Council of Medical Research, Hyderabad. Recommended energy share <55% carbs, 0.83 g/kg protein.
  • NFHS-5 (2019-2021): National Family Health Survey (MOHFW/IIPS), Comprehensive state fact sheets on anemia, stunting, wasting, and adult overweight burden.
  • CNNS (2016-2018): Comprehensive National Nutrition Survey, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW), Government of India and UNICEF. Biochemical micronutrient assessment of Vitamin D, B12, Zinc, and Folate.
